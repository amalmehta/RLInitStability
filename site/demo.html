Summary table

Policy entropy at step 0 shows what the init already decided. ln 2 ≈ 0.69 means a uniform policy. Large or unscaled output weights start out nearly deterministic, so exploration begins from a biased policy.
Dormant and saturated units are capacity the network cannot use: ReLU units that never fire, or tanh units pinned at ±1 where the gradient is near zero (Sokar et al. 2023; Lyle et al. 2024).
Weight norm growth shrinks the effective learning rate and is a classic plasticity-loss symptom (Lyle et al. 2024). Unscaled inits start with a huge norm.
Feature rank (srank) counts how many directions the last hidden layer really uses. Collapse correlates with poor value learning (Kumar et al. 2021; Moalla et al. 2024).

Signal propagation at initialization

Random inputs pass through a deep stack of identical layers before any training. A good init keeps the activation spread and the gradient size roughly constant across depth. A poor one makes them shrink (vanishing) or blow up (exploding) exponentially. Both are drawn on a log scale. The two orthogonal schemes differ only in their output heads, so here they share one line. Some lines overlap on purpose: on square layers Xavier and LeCun have the same variance, and orthogonal with gain √2 matches He's scale.
